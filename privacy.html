QViewing Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Summary

QViewing does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What the app does with data

  • Console connection. QViewing connects directly to a lighting console on your local network using the IP address and port you enter. Cue information received from the console is shown on screen and is not sent anywhere else.
  • Settings. Your console address and display preferences (such as brightness) are saved only on your device.
  • Local Network permission. iOS asks for Local Network access so the app can reach your console. It is used for nothing else.

Third parties

QViewing contains no third-party SDKs and does not send data to the developer or any third party.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
